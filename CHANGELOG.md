# Cinderwing changelog

## 1.6

- Added an in-game scoring guide with enemy values, danger bonuses, chain reactions, and extra-ship thresholds.
- Added rare explosive asteroids with visible fuses and chain reactions. Asteroid Fields continue every ten waves after wave 30.
- Expanded the Asteroid Field art, and added animated departures and arrivals when traveling between star systems.
- Improved player banking, refreshed Reaper's claws and enemy archive entries, and refined Gunwing's dive and return flight.
- Attackers that leave the bottom of the playfield end their pass and re-enter from above. Returning fighters can reclaim an open formation slot clear of black-hole gravity.
- Rebalanced several enemy score values and adjusted player and enemy hull sizes.

## 1.5

- Added a loading screen and an option to hide enemy health and burn bars.
- Improved Ultra Elite formation rejoining and blocked enemy shots from behind Siege Ark toward the player.
- Restored the Space Carrier's standard-run difficulty and improved performance.

## 1.4

- Added rare Ultra Elite Ember, Wasp, and Hunter fighters and more Elite escorts in New Game+ carrier encounters.
- Added lasting, localized hull damage, with smoke and sparks from impact sites and damage that carries over to wrecks and fragments.
- Refined Gunwing and Dreadwing attacks, formation spacing, black-hole avoidance, and shot grazing.
- Added full-size portrait play and adjustable screen shake. Updated fire, explosion audio, carrier exhaust, and nebula effects.

## 1.3

- Improved ship fires, fireballs, light masks, and formation movement.
- Disabled ships carry their momentum into wreck motion. Added full-size portrait support.

## 1.2

- Added portrait rotation, enemy banking, source lighting, and automatic mouse-pointer hiding.
- Updated carrier explosions, hull impacts, asteroid textures, and engine exhaust.
- Improved group attack spacing and firing coordination, and corrected ship-count display.

## 1.1

- Added controller remapping.
- Rebalanced the Brood Carrier, Gunwing, and Dreadwing, and refined other combat effects and timing.

## 1.0

- Initial release.
